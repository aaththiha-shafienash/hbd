Our Little Story

From a classroom to a collection of memories

It all started in the last row of a classroom, where a boy and a girl sat on opposite sides, yet somehow found a reason to talk all day. Between classes, lessons, laughter, and all the little things in between, an ordinary classroom slowly became the beginning of a beautiful friendship.

Soon, sharing Mentos became one of their little things. Sometimes she would offer him a handful, and whenever there were only two pieces left in a packet, they would simply take one each and share it without even thinking twice. Somehow, even the smallest things became memories.

Their conversations slowly became a story of their own. Their chat was filled with endless messages, random conversations, and ridiculously long audio notes—so long that even a one-hour voice note somehow felt completely normal between them. They could talk about almost anything and still somehow have something more to say.

Then came the earphones.

He gifted her a pair of earphones, and for her, they became so much more than just earphones. They became something special, something she valued deeply and kept safely like a little piece of their friendship. Out of all the things she could have received, this became one of those gifts that quietly meant the most.

At school, they would meet in the corridors, casually talking while he carried his Siddalebbe bottle. He knew she hated the smell, which made it even more fun for him to annoy her with it. But the funniest part was that he actually brought that bottle all the way from home to school. Somehow, even his little ways of annoying her became memories she would laugh about later.

Then came the time after O/Ls.

Their friendship didn’t end with school. Somehow, life kept finding ways to put them in the same places. They went to the same classes for A/Ls, and he even became her teacher in P6. Different classrooms, different stages of life, but somehow the same friendship continued through all of them.

There were study moments too. When she was out of town, he would sit down and write notes in her exercise book for her. It was such a simple thing, but it was the kind of help that showed how naturally they looked out for each other.

And then there was the selfie.

For the longest time, taking a picture with him had been something she had wished for since her school days. So when it finally happened, it wasn’t just another selfie. It was one of those small wishes that had quietly waited for the right moment to come true.

Then came the carefree days—the kind where they would simply run together along a long path, laughing and enjoying the moment, all because they wanted to go get some ice cream. Nothing complicated. No special plan. Just two friends having fun and making another memory out of an ordinary day.

Whenever she wanted a photograph, he somehow became the photographer. He would stand behind the camera, making sure she got the picture she wanted, while another little memory was quietly added to their collection.

And then there was the escalator.

She was scared, and he was there to help her get through it. Of course, he didn’t miss the chance to make fun of her while doing it. That was just how their friendship worked—helping each other while never letting the other person live down their embarrassing moments.

Looking back, none of these moments were extraordinary on their own.

A packet of Mentos.
A long voice note.
A pair of earphones.
A silly bottle brought from home.
A few notes written in an exercise book.
A selfie they had waited years to take.
An ice cream run.
A photograph.
A scary escalator ride.

Just ordinary little moments.

But that is what made the friendship special,

and how that simple friendship became a collection of memories worth keeping forever.

Happiest twenty-one
